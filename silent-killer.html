Silent Killer

Television Commercial

Art Director

Awards
Gold VUKA

Challenge: The VUKA Awards is a South African award for non-profits, NGOs and PSA ads. The VUKA Awards have a long standing history of hard-hitting commercials, many of them focusing on the dangers of smoking. All VUKA Award entries are done pro-bono by production companies and young creatives. We partnered with the Heart & Stroke Foundation of South Africa.

Insight: The VUKA Awards had a 2 year break, due to lack of funding. In those 2 years, obesity had surpassed smoking as the leading cause of heart disease. At the time, South Africa was passing stricter regulations on smoking in public, but the issue of rising obesity was not being effectively addressed.

Solution: We created a television ad that highlighted the imbalance between how we regulate health issues by placing them side-by-side. Smoking may be the devil we know, but obesity is the devil we don't: The silent killer.
