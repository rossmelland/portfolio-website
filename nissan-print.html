Nissan Navara

Client
Nissan South Africa
Agency
Straight Twisted
Category
Print
Role
Art Director
Year
2013

Challenge: The brief given by Nissan was to develop print ads for Nissan Navara. No shoot was possible, therefore existing photography had to be used.

Insight: The Nissan Navara is a powerful truck that can handle the toughest conditions. We wanted to encapsulate that spirit of power into something visually striking.

Solution: An image of a charging bull is formed in the dust thrown up behind the Nissan Navara. Half beast. Half you.
