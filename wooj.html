WOOJ came with a clear brand position: they are not dog food. They are a dog treats brand. Only treats. The packaging needed to reflect that personality. Bold, playful and totally distinct from the sea of earnest pet nutrition brands. The result is a vibrant range that lets the product speak for itself.
